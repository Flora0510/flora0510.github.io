One of Dentelle's wings is broken! How will the little tooth fairy collect Nathan’s tooth? Her clumsy assistant, Parmigiano, would like to give her a hand. But is this really a good idea? A charming new reimagining of a classic character with touching themes of friendship, perseverance, and magic!
